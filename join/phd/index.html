How to apply ↓View recruitment poster ↗

Investigate wearable exoskeletons that reduce joint loading during human movement, connecting biomechanics with robotic design and intelligent control.

Research focus

  • Develop models and algorithms for lower-limb joint loading estimation.
  • Design or integrate assistive devices and optimize assistance strategies.
  • Evaluate ideas through simulation and human experiments.

Your contribution

  • Develop estimation and control algorithms.
  • Conduct simulations and analyze experimental data.
  • Work with the research team and contribute to publications.

PREPARATION

What we’re looking for

  • Preparation in mechanical engineering, robotics, biomedical engineering, or a related field.
  • Useful strengths include biomechanics, control, optimization, or machine learning.
  • Programming skills such as Python or MATLAB; practical experience with sensing or wearable robotics is a plus.
  • The advertised profile is a master’s degree or equivalent experience. Formal eligibility is determined by NTU’s admission requirements.

APPLICATION

How to apply

  1. Send an inquiry using the form or email below for an initial discussion of research fit.
  2. Submit a separate formal application through the NTU MAE PhD programme. Indicate Jiaen Wu as your intended supervisor.
