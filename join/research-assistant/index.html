How to apply ↓

Contribute to ongoing work in wearable robotics, biomechanics, sensing, or intelligent control. The project and responsibilities are discussed based on your background and availability.

Research focus

  • Build and test robotic hardware or sensing systems.
  • Develop algorithms, models, simulations, or real-time control.
  • Support experiments and analyze human movement data.

Your contribution

  • Take ownership of agreed technical tasks and document your work.
  • Work with the team to build, test, analyze, and improve research systems.

PREPARATION

What we’re looking for

  • Relevant engineering, robotics, biomechanics, computing, or related technical preparation.
  • Experience in one or more areas such as CAD, embedded systems, control, sensing, data analysis, or Python/MATLAB.
  • Curiosity, responsibility, and a willingness to learn across disciplines.

APPLICATION

How to apply

  1. Explore relevant project topics and identify the skills you would like to contribute.
  2. Send your materials through the inquiry form or email. Include your available start date and intended duration.

Prepare these materials

  • CV.
  • A brief statement of interests, up to one page: background, preferred topics, and availability.
  • Optional: transcript, portfolio, or code/project links.
