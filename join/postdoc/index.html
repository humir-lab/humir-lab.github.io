Wearable exoskeletons for human balance

How to apply ↓View recruitment poster ↗

Develop and evaluate wearable robotic assistance that enhances balance and stability during walking.

Research focus

  • Sense and model human balance and movement.
  • Design wearable exoskeletons and control strategies for balance assistance.
  • Validate assistance through experiments with healthy and clinical populations.

Your contribution

  • Lead independent research and drive project development.
  • Publish research, develop new ideas, and help shape future projects.
  • Mentor students and contribute to proposals and grants.

PREPARATION

What we’re looking for

  • A PhD in mechanical engineering, robotics, biomedical engineering, or a related field, completed or nearing completion.
  • A strong background in control, robotics, biomechanics, or human movement.
  • Experience with wearable robotics, human experiments, or closely related research.
  • Programming skills such as Python or MATLAB.

APPLICATION

How to apply

  1. Prepare your materials and submit an inquiry using the form or email below.
  2. We will discuss research fit and the advertised role.

Prepare these materials

  • CV, including publication links if available.
  • A 1–2 page research statement: key contributions, future direction, and fit with HuMIR.
  • Optional: two representative papers.
