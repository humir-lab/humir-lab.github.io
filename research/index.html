UNDERSTAND HUMAN MOVEMENT

The dynamics
of balance.

Can we recognize and predict instability before balance is lost?

We combine biomechanics, wearable sensing, and learning to characterize gait, estimate human motion, and investigate future balance states.

  • Balance assessment and movement prediction
  • Wearable sensing and sensor fusion
  • Vision-based locomotion and intent recognition

ENHANCE HUMAN CAPABILITY

Working with
the body.

How can wearable robots support balance and reduce joint loading?

We connect robotic hardware with biomechanically informed control to develop assistance that adapts to the person and the movement task.

  • Powered exoskeletons and real-time control
  • Adaptive assistance and human–robot interaction
  • Joint loading reduction and balance augmentation

CURRENTLY IN DEVELOPMENT

Powered prosthetics

We are developing powered ankle and knee–ankle prostheses that bring together mechanical design, sensing, and adaptive control to support walking.

  • Actuation and transmission design
  • Gait-phase estimation and embedded sensing
  • Coordinated joint control and adaptive assistance
