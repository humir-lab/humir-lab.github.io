PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR

Jiaen Wu

Assistant Professor
School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, NTU

Jiaen leads the Human Movement Intelligence and Robotics Lab. Her research connects biomechanics, wearable robotics, and human-centered intelligence to understand, enhance, and restore human movement.

Before joining NTU in September 2026, she was a postdoctoral researcher at Stanford University with Steve Collins. She received her PhD from ETH Zürich in 2022, working with Bradley Nelson in the Multi-Scale Robotics Lab, and her bachelor’s degree in Control Engineering from Zhejiang University in 2017.

Background & recognition

Jiaen was a Wu Tsai Human Performance Alliance Distinguished Postdoctoral Fellow at Stanford and an MIT Rising Star in Mechanical Engineering (2025). Her work has been translated into a certified medical device, recognized with second place in the Swiss AI Awards (2022), and selected as a finalist for the Swiss Excellence Product Award (2022). Her research was featured among Parkinson’s News Today’s “Top 10 Parkinson’s stories of 2024.”
